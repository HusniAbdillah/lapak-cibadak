# 01. Project Brief: Lapak Cibadak

## 1.1. Executive Summary
**Project Name:** Lapak Cibadak  
**Core Theme:** "Karya Lokal, Cerita Cibadak" (Aligned with KKNT program: *cibadak.bercerita*)  
**Primary Focus:** Digital Directory, Storytelling, and Business Profiling  
**Target Location:** Desa Cibadak, Ciampea, Kabupaten Bogor, Indonesia  

Lapak Cibadak is a progressive web application (PWA) designed to serve as a comprehensive digital directory for over 100 Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (UMKM) in Desa Cibadak. Moving away from a strict e-commerce catalog model, this platform prioritizes the **Business Profile and Narrative**. Recognizing that many local businesses (e.g., tailors, repair shops, traditional food stalls) do not have structured product inventories, the system treats specific product listings as entirely optional. The primary goal is to map the village's economic potential, tell the stories behind the entrepreneurs, and provide direct communication channels (WhatsApp) for prospective buyers.

## 1.2. Design Identity & Brand Voice
The platform utilizes an **Elegant Brutalist** design system tailored for the Indonesian micro-business sector[cite: 1]. It bridges the gap between raw, structural integrity and high-end editorial clarity[cite: 1].
*   **Aesthetic & Vibe:** The design evokes a sense of "Structured Reliability"[cite: 1]. By removing soft shadows and roundedness, the UI feels architectural and permanent[cite: 1].
*   **Color Palette:** The palette is rooted in a traditional yet high-contrast foundation[cite: 1]. It utilizes an Ivory base (`#fff8f0`), Deep Navy/Royal Blue for primary elements (`#0e1743`, `#4b57a9`), and Gold (`#f2bf48`) for critical highlights[cite: 1]. 
*   **Typography:** The design dictates a massive hierarchy, utilizing **Space Grotesk** for heavy, uppercase headlines with tight tracking, and **Hanken Grotesk** for clean, legible body text[cite: 1].
*   **Shapes:** A universal 0px radius is applied to every UI component, ensuring sharp, geometric lines across all cards and buttons[cite: 1].

## 1.3. Core Objectives & Key Results (OKRs)
1.  **Digital Profiling:** Successfully index 100+ UMKM with rich profiles including background stories, established years, and exact geolocations.
2.  **Frictionless Lead Generation:** Route visitors directly to UMKM owners via pre-filled WhatsApp links, acting as a lead-generation tool rather than a checkout cart.
3.  **Community Sourcing:** Enable a zero-friction, login-free submission portal for villagers to propose new business entries or edit existing ones.
4.  **Data Integrity:** Protect the live database from vandalism through a robust, admin-controlled "Draft-Publish" moderation pipeline.
